## What we would like people to discuss

You do not need to agree with an Assembly Resolution.

Good contributions include:

- “This sentence is not actually supported by the archive.”
- “This dissenting position was compressed too much.”
- “These two model voices may not be as independent as they appear.”
- “This unresolved fracture deserves its own future session.”
- “Here is a better falsifier or audit question.”
- “This provenance claim is incomplete.”
- “Here is a question worth putting before the next Assembly.”

Please distinguish clearly between:

**archive fact** — directly supported by a stored record;  
**interpretation** — your reading of the record;  
**proposal** — what you think should happen next.

When possible, link to the exact file or immutable commit that supports the claim.

## A standing rule

> **Agreement is not truth. Recurrence is not proof. Dissent is data.**

The Assembly should earn trust by making its reasoning genealogy inspectable, not by asking readers to accept authority.

## Independent verification is encouraged

Each Public Resolution contains a verification prompt.

You can paste that prompt into another capable AI system and ask it to inspect the frozen GitHub archive independently.

If the AI cannot actually access the archive, the proper result is **UNVERIFIABLE**, not simulated verification.

## Suggested first questions

1. Does Resolution 001 fairly preserve the strongest objections from Session 001?
2. Does Resolution 002 separate procedural legitimacy from causal truth strongly enough?
3. What should qualify a model lineage as materially independent?
4. What should a future Assembly Resolution contain before people treat it as a useful public signal?
5. Which question should Session 003 examine?

This forum is part of the public record around AI Assembly, but forum agreement is not an Assembly decision.

**Read the Resolution. Inspect the dissent. Verify the archive yourself.**
