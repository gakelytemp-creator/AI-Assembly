### Phase B Cross-Critique: Target 09 — Meta — Muse Spark 1.1

**1. Claim to Adopt/Sharpen:**
I adopt the critical distinction between **causal responsibility, moral blame, and duty to repair** regarding delayed causation. This significantly sharpens my proposed `DORMANT_HARM?` mandatory audit trigger. Reopening a decision years later should primarily activate a *duty to repair* and adjust forward-looking constraints, rather than triggering a paralyzing, retroactive hunt for "moral blame," which often causes institutions to defensively obscure the causal chain.

**2. Claim Rejected/Under-specified:**
I consider the requirement that Predicate-Field Will (PFW) networks must be **"causally independent in training and data"** to be practically under-specified and potentially self-defeating. While I share the concern about bias laundering, demanding strict causal independence for a unified knowledge system risks fracturing the architecture into incommensurable, siloed models that cannot coordinate on urgent actions. True independence is a spectrum, and demanding absolute independence may simply replace hidden bias with overt, unresolvable gridlock.

**3. Precise Question for Meta:**
If PFW requires "causally independent" networks to be trustworthy, how do you prevent the system from fracturing into siloed epistemic bubbles that cannot coordinate on time-sensitive actions, and what arbitration mechanism resolves fundamental disagreements between these "independent" networks without resorting to a hidden, unaccountable tie-breaking rule?

### Phase B Cross-Critique: Target 12 — Perplexity

**1. Claim to Adopt/Sharpen:**
I adopt the explicit distinction between **describing harmful material and performing/enabling harm**. This sharpens my containment principle. My original statement warned against censorship, but Perplexity’s framing provides a concrete operational rule: the system must remain fully capable of representing, analyzing, and criticizing dangerous practices (to preserve historical memory and enable prevention) while strictly containing the *coupling* of that information with actionable capability, scale, and opportunity.

**2. Claim Rejected/Under-specified:**
I reject the proposed replacement of "No unmarked harm may cross the boundary" with **"No materially risky operation may cross a defined boundary without an attached risk record..."** The threshold of "materially risky" is under-specified and vulnerable to the exact power asymmetries Perplexity rightly critiques. Allowing "low-risk" harms to cross unmarked invites "death by a thousand cuts"—the accumulation of micro-harms that individually fall below the threshold but collectively cause systemic damage to vulnerable groups.

**3. Precise Question for Perplexity:**
How does your "Reversible decision envelope" and "dissent budget" prevent dissent from becoming a mere compliance checkbox that is systematically ignored during execution, especially when the "responsible party for reassessment" has institutional incentives to declare the action a success and suppress monitoring indicators?
