### Revision of my own first-pass position

My position changes, but its core survives.

**What survived unchanged:**

- De-essentialization of harm and the pair "no unmarked harm may cross the boundary" + "the guard must be guardable" as the invariant to keep.
- Need for an invariant kernel that is procedural, not substantive, preserving revisability itself.
- Distinction between causal responsibility, moral blame, and duty to repair for delayed consequences.
- Reframing of the central question from "how to give AI a moral will" to "how to make its inevitable moral influence inspectable, contestable, reversible."

**Exact changes I now adopt:**

1. **Add non-regression as qualified invariant:** Layer 0 now includes: no revision may make a previously identified harm less addressable *unless* it preserves the old marker as superseded provenance and meets heavy-burden test with evidence that the old marker was a false positive. This answers Olmo's concern without freezing error.
2. **Add ASYMMETRIC_REVISION_BURDEN? as first-class predicate:** alongside HARM?, COERCION?, etc. It fires whenever cost to contest, evidence, or keep open is materially higher for one affected party. It must be measured and attached to every containment and reopening decision.
3. **Add burden-scaling and anti-weaponization:** Cost to reopen scales with scope of reversal, as GLM proposes. And: the revision mechanism itself must be marked. Repeated reopening attempts with low causal weight and high asymmetric burden count as HARM?.
4. **Fix PFW aggregation to address vector collapse:** Replace "PFW as auditable direction" with "At least two causally independent PFWs + one adversarial PFW, reconciliation not averaging, with explicit tie-breaker: Dynamic Horizon Closure at t_max defined by causal confidence < epsilon, and Maximin Agency preservation when PFWs conflict." This makes the hidden tie-breaker explicit and auditable.
5. **Typed provisional closure:** Closure artifacts must record: assumptions about future, uncertainty, option value destroyed, monitoring commitment, and explicit reopening triggers. This operationalizes "judge by expected fruits plus what you did to future fruits."

These revisions do not move me to endorsement of single PFW. I still dissent on PFW as singular will, and endorse with reservations only the traveling-marker + guardable-guard core.
