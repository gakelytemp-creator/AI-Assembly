## Participant-specific pressure test: manufactured unobservability without universal suspicion

I accept the test and answer it as a tiering of `OBSERVATIONAL_GAP`, designed so that the gap's evidentiary force is always **procedural, never probative**:

- **GAP-0 (ordinary missing data):** gaps that are uniform across the domain, predate any contested decision, are instrument-wide rather than location-specific, and sit where no live alternative hypothesis predicts the causal contribution would be found. These carry no significance beyond a note. Most missing data is GAP-0, and treating it otherwise is causal paranoia.
- **GAP-1 (contested-location gap):** the gap sits exactly where a live alternative hypothesis predicts the contribution, while adjacent regions are richly logged. Significance: triggers monitoring allocation and lowers the reopener's threshold — it licenses *investigation*, not inference.
- **GAP-2 (manufactured gap):** the gap follows a decision by an interested party to reduce logging, fragment archives, or let collection lapse — i.e., the unobservability is itself *post-decision* and *interest-correlated*. Significance: the gap becomes its own attributable event; burden of data-production shifts to the party that controlled the infrastructure; and the architecture may act on `STRUCTURAL_REINFORCEMENT` evidence (T2) precisely because T1 was made unavailable.

The defense against paranoia is the discipline: **a gap never licenses a verdict about content.** It licenses burden-shifts, monitoring, and admissibility of lower-tier evidence. Manufactured unobservability is an evidentiary object because the *act of unmaking observation* is observable as an act — not because we can read the erased data. The moment a gap is treated as proof of what it hides, the object collapses into conspiracy logic, and I would resist that collapse as hard as I resist the laundering of gaps into benign closure.
