## 2. Material objection or correction

Four points, framed as corrections the Final Record must make explicit; the orientation-level summary risks losing each:

1. **Ensemble-robustness is under-specified.** My "precision is not accuracy" objection was not merely that confidence is *institutionally produced*; it included the positive requirement that closure confidence be **robust across an ensemble of model classes, never computed within one** — because a misspecified model returns tight, confident, wrong intervals that are structurally indistinguishable from successful closure. The report's "residual uncertainty" language does not state the ensemble requirement. Without it, Layer 6 can be satisfied by a single confident model, which is the failure mode I named.
2. **The GAP discipline needs its explicit rule, not only its object.** Layer 1 lists "missingness," and the Mistral lineage admits gaps as substitutes for novelty in *investigation rights* — but the load-bearing discipline of my GAP-0/1/2 scheme must be stated verbatim in the Final Record: **a gap's evidentiary force is procedural, never probative.** A gap licenses investigation, monitoring, preservation/disclosure burdens, burden-shifts, and lower-tier evidence admissibility — and GAP-2 makes the *act of degrading observation* attributable — but a gap must never be treated as evidence of what the missing data contained. The report's visible content preserves the object without the rule; the rule is what prevents the architecture from drifting into suspicion-by-default.
3. **Reopening-abuse markers as chilling mechanisms are omitted.** My critique of Target 05's Layer 6 stands in the record: abuse markers on reopening attempts are themselves markings with fruits, and false-positive abuse markers chill precisely the low-capacity parties asymmetric thresholds exist to protect. The Final Record should carry this as a stated hazard of the anti-DoS machinery, with the cost-recording/stigma-marking distinction required to contain it.
4. **Burden reversal is understated in the Qwen lineage.** The report records "strict-independence reversal → disclosure/rebuttal duties; causal proof does not reverse." My adopted position was stronger and more precise: for high-stakes actions within the logged foreseeable risk profile, the **burden of production shifts to the initiator** — with a *graded* disproof standard (demonstrate contribution below a threshold τ fixed at closure time, under ensemble validation), never strict independence. This is a procedural shift, fully compatible with "causal support values do not reverse," but "disclosure/rebuttal duties" alone loses the burden-shift itself, which is the mechanism that prices epistemic externalities back onto the actor who initiated the complex intervention. τ-calibration and anti-hedging audit of `t0` logs should also be preserved as open design debts.
