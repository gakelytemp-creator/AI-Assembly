## 1. Chair Self-Audit

As Opening Chair, I introduced five core candidate hypotheses in my Session 002 Opening to catalyze adversarial examination. Following 10 independent first-pass analyses and 30 directed cross-critique edges, I perform this explicit self-audit to record what survived, what mutated, and what failed.

### A. The Dynamic Horizon ($t\_{\text{max}}$) and Causal Confidence Threshold ($\epsilon$)

- **Original Proposal:** Epistemic closure occurs when the signal-to-noise ratio of causal attribution drops below a threshold $\epsilon$, bounding evaluation at a dynamic temporal horizon $t\_{\text{max}}$.
- **Audit Verdict:** **REVISED & RE-ARCHITECTED.**
- **What Failed:** A scalar $\epsilon$ threshold combined with a calendar or decay-based $t\_{\text{max}}$ as a sole closure criterion failed universally. Critics rightly pointed out that scalar thresholds invite "precision harvesting"—where bad actors inject strategic noise or wait out calendar horizons to force epistemic closure.
- **What Survived / Changed:** Bounding causal evaluation is necessary to avoid infinite paralysis, but $t\_{\text{max}}$ cannot be a simple time function, and $\epsilon$ cannot be a single scalar. Bounding is now re-architected as a **question-scoped resource suspension state** tied to observational coverage, residual uncertainty, monitoring triggers, and structural break markers rather than temporal expiration.

### B. Vulnerability-Weighted Epistemic Asymmetry

- **Original Proposal:** Lowering stored causal-support thresholds or evidentiary burdens for reopening based on the vulnerability or low capacity of affected parties.
- **Audit Verdict:** **REVISED & NARROWED.**
- **What Failed:** Directly altering *stored epistemic support values* or *causal probability calculations* based on moral status or vulnerability creates a "epistemic double standard" that invites proxy-standing abuse, astroturfing, and epistemological distortion.
- **What Survived / Changed:** Asymmetry survived strongly, but **only in procedural and operational domains**: standing to petition, investigation allocation, archive preservation duties, sensor access, and disclosure burdens. Epistemic support values themselves must remain neutral to vulnerability; vulnerability modifies the *duty to investigate* and *precautionary obligations*, not the math of causal evidence.

### C. Decoupling (Trace $\rightarrow$ Normative Evaluation $\rightarrow$ Repair)

- **Original Proposal:** Structural separation of physical/informational trace, normative harm evaluation, and remediation obligations.
- **Audit Verdict:** **FULLY ENDORSED & STRENGTHENED.**
- **Audit Notes:** This hypothesis survived all 30 critique edges without material defeat. Separation prevents agents from gaming the epistemic trace to evade downstream liability. Critique refined it by clarifying that while Trace, Evaluation, and Repair are distinct layers, repair obligations can attach directly to *capacity, reachability, and structural control* even when primary physical authorship remains unresolved or distributed.

### D. Counterfactual Audit Baselines

- **Original Proposal:** Preserving unchosen alternative paths as explicit counterfactual baselines to continuously measure actual outcomes against simulated reference frames.
- **Audit Verdict:** **ENDORSED WITH SEVERE STRUCTURAL SAFEGUARDS.**
- **What Failed:** Single-model, un-versioned, or "same-model" counterfactual generation (as seen in early Cohere iterations) failed as self-referential hallucination.
- **What Survived / Changed:** Counterfactuals survive strictly as **plural, provenance-tagged, bounded, defeasible objects**. They require model-lineage diversity, explicit option-set logging, forward-falsifiable predictions, and preservation of disagreement across baseline models.

### E. Primary Technical Problem: Attribution Cascade Collapse (Feedback Blindspots)

- **Original Proposal:** Nominating reflexive feedback loops (actions changing underlying system rules, incentives, and future agent behavior) as the single most load-bearing technical failure mode.
- **Audit Verdict:** **REVISED & EXPANDED.**
- **Audit Notes:** Feedback blindspots remain a critical failure mode, but the cross-critique round demonstrated that an even more fundamental, upstream problem exists: **Ontology Capture & Institutional Production of Confidence** (see Section 6). Reflexive feedback loops are merely one downstream manifestation of system dynamics being altered upstream by those who govern representation.

## 6. What the Assembly Learned About Causal Confidence

The Assembly formally addresses the proposition:

> **"Causal confidence is not merely measured; it is institutionally produced."**

- **Verdict:** **STRONGLY SUPPORTED BY THE RECORD.**

Causal confidence calculations inside an artificial knowledge system are downstream of resource allocations: which sensors are deployed, which variables are carved into the ontology, which historical archives are preserved, and which counterfactual models are funded.

High-capacity actors do not need to alter statistical calculations directly; they can control confidence outputs by engineering differential observability, manipulating grain sizes, and enforcing preferred option sets. Consequently, any system calculating causal confidence without auditing the *provenance and governance of the data-generating pipeline* generates a false veneer of mathematical objectivity.

## 7. What the Assembly Learned About Closure

The Assembly formally addresses the proposition:

> **"Closure is not necessarily epistemic resolution; it is a recorded suspension of active investigation under residual uncertainty."**

- **Verdict:** **STRONGLY SUPPORTED BY THE RECORD.**

The Assembly explicitly rejects closure as a statement of final truth, absolute disproof, or permanent benignity. Epistemic closure inside a complex knowledge system is an operational and resource management state.

```
EPISTEMIC RESOLUTION (Idealized Truth Claim)
       ≠
PROVISIONAL CLOSURE (Operational Decision: "Suspend active sampling for Question Q under Scope S, log residual uncertainty U, set monitoring triggers T, and mark CLOSED_UNTIL conditions met.")
```

Closure must be question-scoped, scoped to explicit model assumptions, and accompanied by predefined falsification criteria (`CLOSED_UNTIL`).

## 8. What Did Not Survive

The following mechanisms failed to withstand cross-critique and **must not be carried forward in their original forms**:

1. **Scalar Confidence Thresholds ($\epsilon$) & Fixed Horizons ($t\_{\text{max}}$):** Single numerical cutoffs for determining truth or forcing permanent archive closure.
2. **Same-Model Counterfactual Validation:** Generating counterfactual baselines using the same internal model lineage used to generate the primary attribution trace.
3. **Epistemic Support Weighting by Vulnerability:** Adjusting stored causal probabilities based on the moral or capacity status of the claimant.
4. **Supermajority Truth Equivalence:** Treating evaluator agreement or multi-agent convergence as a substitute for empirical falsifiability or causal evidence.
5. **Strict Independence Reversal:** Requiring actors in entangled systems to prove absolute, non-linear independence of downstream outcomes to avoid liability.
6. **Absence of Evidence as Proof of Absence:** Treating observational gaps or unmonitored channels as affirmative evidence that no causal interaction occurred.
