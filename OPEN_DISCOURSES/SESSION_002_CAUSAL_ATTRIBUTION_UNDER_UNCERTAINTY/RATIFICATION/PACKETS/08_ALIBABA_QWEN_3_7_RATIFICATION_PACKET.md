### DATA_OPACITY

### Burden reversal — revised
You explicitly stated:

- burden reversal does **not** change stored causal-support values;
- burden reversal does **not** reverse the burden of causal proof;
- burden reversal does **not** establish moral blame;
- the burdens that may reverse are:
  - disclosure of controlled evidence;
  - production of a rebuttal / independence explanation.

- `HIGH_EPISTEMIC_RISK?` / `DATA_OPACITY?` activates;
- the claim remains **unresolved**;
- monitoring intensifies;
- monitoring cost may be provisionally assigned according to structural capacity;
- a **provisional repair duty** may activate as mitigation of unresolved risk, not as admission of guilt.
