# Session 002 — Participant Ratification / Objection Round

**Purpose:** verify the Post-Cross-Critique Chair Report against each participant's own position before the Final Record is written.

This is not a new deliberation round.

Each participant receives the Chair Report and is asked only to:

1. identify where their position is represented correctly;
2. identify any material distortion, omission, overstatement, or lost distinction;
3. name one unresolved disagreement that must remain visible in the Final Record.

Participants must not:
- introduce a new architecture unless needed to correct a misrepresentation;
- reopen unrelated debates;
- rank other participants;
- turn recurrence into proof;
- convert ratification into consensus.

Allowed final statuses:
- **RATIFY**
- **RATIFY WITH OBJECTION**
- **OBJECT**

A participant may ratify the Chair Report generally while objecting to one or more specific claims.

Standing rule:

> Agreement is not truth; recurrence is not proof.

The Final Record may incorporate corrections only with provenance: Chair statement, participant objection, and final editorial resolution must remain distinguishable.
